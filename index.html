Hardware

Right now that means the drop foot brace, a wearable brace that lifts the foot while walking for someone with drop foot. A force sensor on the thigh tells an Arduino Nano where the leg is in its stride, and two servos pull a cord to raise the foot at the right moment.

It has been through 3 prototypes and testing on a real person, and I'm still iterating. I like the whole stack: picking parts, wiring, writing firmware, and tracking down problems like a 5 V rail brownout.

I've also done CAD in Fusion 360 for a lab automation pipetting gantry, moving a telescopic pipette arm onto an X-rail gantry.

Planes
